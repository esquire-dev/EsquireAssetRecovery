Core Explanation: When a property is sold at a foreclosure auction, it may sell for more than the debt owed. That extra amount—known as surplus funds or excess proceeds—legally belongs to the former homeowner.

Esquire's Commitment: We research your claim, explain your legal rights in plain language, and ensure your claim is properly filed to recover the maximum amount you are legally entitled to receive.

### Core Modules
Our Legal-Grade Recovery Process: Recovering surplus funds is a court-regulated legal process requiring petitions, affidavits, identity verification, and strict compliance. One missed form or deadline can delay or forfeit your claim. Esquire manages eligibility verification, document assembly, court filings, agency communications, and advocacy until funds are released.
Why Surplus Funds Go Unclaimed: Systemic barriers like outdated address records, obscure notices, and hard-to-interpret government rules leave millions sitting untouched in court accounts. Many homeowners assume that foreclosure eliminates all equity—when surplus funds are still legally waiting.
A Dedicated Advocate for Your Recovery: Most people fail to claim funds not due to ineligibility, but due to lack of step-by-step guidance. Esquire acts as a dedicated partner, managing research, filings, and court communications while explaining everything in plain language.
Why Clients Trust Esquire: Combining professional legal expertise with genuine compassion, Esquire operates without upfront costs, ensures strict confidentiality, and maintains clear communication throughout the process.
Clear, Legal-Based Guidance: We demystify complex terminology and county procedures into clear, actionable steps so clients feel confident and informed.

### Additional Modules
Transparent Communication & Clear Guidance: Unclear instructions and unresponsive offices can make recovery feel exhausting. We provide consistent updates, explain requirements in plain language, and clarify what the court is reviewing at each stage. You will know what is happening, what we need from you, and what comes next—without confusion or pressure.
What Makes Esquire Different: Unlike services that offer minimal guidance or rely on automated systems, we review your situation thoroughly and explain the details with clarity. Experienced attorneys, auditors, and investigators handle research, filings, and communication with county and state agencies. We protect your rights, look out for your best interests, and guide recovery with diligence, respect, and accountability.
Eligibility Review: A Simple First Step: Many people assume they do not qualify, when eligibility is often easier to determine than expected. A no-cost review requires only minimal information. After evaluating your situation, we explain what we find in clear language and answer questions without pressure. Even if you are unsure whether surplus funds exist, a brief conversation can uncover options you did not know were available.

### Recovery Workflow
Optional — Free Eligibility Review: A brief, no-cost conversation requiring minimal information to evaluate case records and confirm whether recoverable funds exist.
Step 1 — Comprehensive Claim Investigation: Detailed review of foreclosure records, auction results, and county filings to confirm surplus amounts, check for competing liens, and verify legal standing.
Step 2 — Court-Ready Filings & Legal Preparation: Assembly and submission of complete claim packages—including court-ready petitions, affidavits, and supporting evidence—coordinated directly with court clerks.
Step 3 — Final Release & Secure Disbursement: Active oversight of county disbursement procedures following court approval to ensure funds are delivered safely and securely to the client.
